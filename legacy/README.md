# The Bean Professor

I want to make an app that would help me with two specific, but complex functions

- pull from the top barista champions in the world to make recommendations about how to brew my coffee based on my available coffee brewing devices and the specific sing-origin/blend that I have

- pull from top coffee roasters in the world to make recommendations about how to roast my coffee based on the roast profile I am going for and the best way to bring the most flavor out of the beans based on their origin and regional characteristics

This app should also prioritize mobile-friendly usage. Additionally, it will allow users to create an account so they can write and save notes on each brewing and/or roasting session. 

I want to call the app "The Bean Professor" and the appearance should convey the essence of a science lab and a coffee shop.
